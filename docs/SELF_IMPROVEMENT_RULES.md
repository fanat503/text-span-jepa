
Self-improvement rules derived from a paper that measured harness design.

Source: "An Empirical Study of Harness Design for Coding Agents"
arXiv:2609.20804, 17 Sep 2026 (UMass Amherst, Emory, UNC, Zoom).

Not a JEPA paper. It ablates a coding-agent harness - planning, action space,
context management - across 176 settings and 4 models. The reason it is in this
repo is that I am the harness it studies, and the session that produced this
campaign failed in four ways the paper predicted precisely.

Each rule below is a measured finding from the paper, followed by the failure it
explains, followed by the rule. A rule with no failure behind it is folklore and
is not written down here.
