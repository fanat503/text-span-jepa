finding: `baselines/mlm_baseline.py:5-7` states a "Fair comparison guarantee: identical model capacity (encoder params match exactly), identical compute (same FLOPs per forward pass), only the training objective differs" — both of the two quantitative halves are false, and the false claim sits in the header of the file whose entire purpose is to be the fair control.
evidence: measured in-process (meta-free, vocab-exact via a two-point linear fit in V, `max_seq_len=512`):
  MLM 640/10 rung (`config/wikitext/mlm_wikitext_small.yaml`): P(V) = 1280*V + 49,564,160 -> **113,953,280** params, all trainable.
  JEPA 640/10 rung (`config/scaling/small_100m.yaml:20-21`, measured by the perf audit): total **170,706,561**, trainable **88,947,841**.
  -> JEPA total is **1.498x** the baseline; the baseline's *trainable* count is **1.281x** JEPA's. Neither equals "identical capacity". The gap is structural, not a rounding issue: JEPA carries a frozen `target_encoder` deepcopy (81,958,720 params at 640/10) plus predictor and decoder; the baseline carries one `mlm_head` (640*50304 = 32,194,560).
  Compute: `mlm_baseline.py:76-78` projects **all** B*T positions (`logits = self.mlm_head(h)` -> (B,T,V)) and only then gathers masked rows at :100. `src/models/jepa.py:716-718` runs its decoder on `h_online[mask_positions.bool()]` only. At the matched micro-batch B=64, T=512, V=50304, fp32: dense (B,T,V) = **6.14 GiB**, JEPA's masked-only logits at its 0.35 mask_ratio = **2.15 GiB** — a 2.86x activation-memory penalty on the baseline, on top of a different FLOP count. data2vec is unaffected: `data2vec_baseline.py:134` also gathers before the head.
